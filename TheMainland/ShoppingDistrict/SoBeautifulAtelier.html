So Beautiful Atelier. For the unacquainted, it's a clothing store for mainlanders that sells nice, quality clothes with aesthetics aspirational of Luxizor - meant to be attainable for Mainlanders, though, attainable in the sense that you might own two or three pieces from it, not stuff your entire wardrobe. The clothes range from trendy to professional, and the racks are sparse - only one of each product is put on display. At this level of detail, it would be far too costly to produce ready-to-wear copies. The idea is that you choose the piece you like and get it made to fit. Because beyond the brand-new, flashy clothes, So Beautiful Atelier is foremostly a tailoring business. If you need clothing fitted or repaired at an affordable cost, you've come to the right place. It makes sense, in a world where resources are scarce, that in order to retain customer loyalty, a product should last for life. ...That being said, the So Beautiful Atelier is open, and Jia Li is currently tending to the shop.
