It will be updated accordingly as time moves forward. ⇢ THE HISTORY On the planet Aṇḍa, conflicts over resources began to rise which marked the start of The Great Resource War. The world was struck by a series of nuclear weapons causing mass destruction all over. This heavily reduced the population which led to widespread understaffing of nuclear power plants. Most of the land became an irradiated wasteland, and artifacts of the Old World were destroyed.  Recovering the remnants of old civilization became near impossible, however it did not mean that humanity gave up. There were few survivors that prevailed through the disasters located near Saṃśraya. There were safe levels of radiation: an inhabitable space for the human race to start over. Survivors used this spot to create the first Safe Zone, the largest of the two. They would make shelters, ration their food, and gather resources. Many fell victim to what modern society would call "blights"— decaying and distorted animated bodies that refuse to die. This caused a need for more organized efforts to preserve life, thus Luxizor was formed by the wealthy of the Old World. They began to capitalize on necessities, producing items such as gas masks, prosthetics, and medicines in exchange for others' services. This system continued to develop and change with new leadership as years passed on. Soon, Luxizor would manage to be in control of most resources such as food, clothes, and other utilities you could imagine. Laws began to be instated to ensure appropriate usage of resources which would then extend into territories such as social expectations. The modern world as we know it operates under a corpocracy led by the current owner of Luxizor, Jared. (edited)

⇢ LIFE ON THE TRACKS The Tracks is a tight-knit community, surviving off of pure goodwill. Think of it as a small town or even a village where everyone is aware of each other, their parents, and the people before them. Because of the neglect from Luxizor, trackers have no one to depend on but one another. Since they have no access to real hospitals or other emergency services, many individuals step up to the plate, volunteering to be aid. When children's parents pass away, the entire community gathers around to help raise them. Nobody has to be alone. Growing up, children in The Tracks play games such as Soldiers and Blights— Similar to Cops and Robbers, but there's a lot more biting and fighting involved. Despite the adults' attempts to ban this game for safety reasons, it always makes a comeback in every generation. Tracker children will often play pretend as well. House is popular, although they often act out the idea of being Mainlanders, pretending to have luxuries and traditional dynamics they're not familiar with. "Let me turn on the stove for you, honey!" and "I will feed the giraffe before I go to work!" So on and so forth. Trackers do not have access to education either. Many are unable to read and write. Most of their knowledge about the world comes from their own lived experiences as well as ancestors'. The Tracks, to this day, do their best to keep their spirits and community alive and thriving. Every night, some of the adults gather around a bonfire and sing songs while they're there. The common people are Julius, Andrew, Daki, and Clay (NPC-Wise). Sometimes they are visited by Dae-hyun and Marianna. (edited)

⇢ THE PARTIERS' GENERATION The generation before this, aging from 21 to 31, was known to be the workers' generation. They were defined by the common-people's mindset that if they pulled themselves up by their bootstraps and grinded, they would be able to make a better life for themselves and the people around them. There was a sense of optimism behind their hard work. The next generation, aging from 14 to 21, are known as the partiers' generation. Inflicted with nihilism due to the false hopes of the previous generation, bettering the world back to its old state, they exhibit more hedonistic tendencies than the last. "Our world is shit, there's nothing we can do about it, so why don't we just enjoy it?" The scene for parties were established 12 years ago; a studious, 10 year-old Rae created a hydroelectric system that powers the sound and lights in the cathedral's bunker. The scene began to popularize gradually over the years since this creation— People began to move some of the decor on the surface of the cathedral down to its bunker, such as the altar where people can often be found being... intimate! More and more mainlanders would find themselves crossing the bridge to take part in the nightly festivities of the cathedral. It became its own place of unity— a shared belief that pleasure is essential in life. There's a culture of dance that began to form as well— a means to communicate with your body. Some call it a call and response; others could find humor in coining it as a mating call. Sometimes, a formation called The Split occurs. It looks like a dance battle at first, but there is no battle, just an olive branch extending to the other side. Because of the world's different dance styles, The Split becomes a time where people can understand one another and be understood in return. Thank you to Milou for this idea! ⇢ JOBS Jobs are mostly offered through Luxizor. There are some smaller independent merchandisers, although they are more “night market” adjacent than actual storefront. Lots of small businesses run out of homes. I will not allow players to play scavengers, but they will be listed. AGRICULTURALISTS
  • Luxizor was able to find cultivable land, creating a second safe zone around it and using a hydroponics system to maintain it. Because this is a dangerous job that requires you to travel through safe zones, the job is assigned to debtors and criminals. This is a high risk and low reward occupation.
MINERS
  • Similar to those who work in agriculture, this job is often assigned to debtors and criminals as it requires you to work in the deadzones. This is a high risk and low reward occupation.
SCAVENGERS
  • An illegal occupation of high risk and high reward. Scavengers travel the dead zones for a number of reasons, whether it be by commission or resource gathering. 
  • Commission denotes that civilians may seek out a scavenger’s service to tour the dead zones. Scavengers offering this service can often be found in the black market. Resource gathering includes collecting… well, useful and valuable resources, but also artifacts. These items often get sold in the black market as well.
  • This is not a popular line of work as the majority of scavengers don’t make it out alive whether that be due to blights, radiation sickness, or trespassing. 
SOLDIERS
  •  The occupying military force. They often flex between several duties that regard the protection of the safe zone’s civilians and maintaining control over resources.
  •  There is routine patrol, where soldiers make themselves accessible around the Mainland for civilian reports. They also keep a lookout for crimes within their vicinity. 
  • Soldiers also retain the ability to seize and search properties of those who are believed to be guilty of suspicious activity, mostly scavenging. 
  • There are few groups who are entrusted with the duty of gathering resources in the deadzones; they are required to perform a search of the provided route and bring back required resources. They are kept in check by border patrol, a group of dedicated soldiers that ensure that resource-gatherers do not bring in contraband. For those who commit this crime, their permits to search the deadzones are revoked as well as suspension from the job.
  • Border patrol also entails those who keep a watch over the safe zone’s walls. They are required to terminate outsiders with no legal permits on sight.
SCIENTISTS
  • Scientists is the broad term we are using to describe those in the science and medical fields.
  • Although they have their main fields of study (biology, chemistry, medicine, etc…), they should maintain an eclectic knowledge of the other sciences. Due to understaffing, it is likely that they will have to flex between various jobs.
  • They will still mostly be assigned jobs that pertain to their main field of study, but for max utilization, it is good to be adaptable.
