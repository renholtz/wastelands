COOL: Roma absolutely appears calm in high-stakes situations, but that is more-so from the fact that she has become pretty detached from her own life, mostly running on autopilot. It's pretty hard to go into a panic when you are oftentimes only half-parsing what is happening around you in order to save energy. She has seen her share of dire emergencies during her time as a volunteer worker, and has been able to learn how to keep a level head when things get tough over the last few years. However, her buttons could absolutely be pressed to send her off the edge if the situation got too personal. Her presence is much more of a calming one than one that outright commands; she would step up to bat to try and lead if absolutely necessary, although she would absolutely have trouble being properly assertive. CHARM: She does not feel particularly happy forcing her will onto people, but if she feels that it is for the greater good or it would make them happy to hear, Roma can easily lie to a person's face. She has a pretty strong intuition for knowing what people want to hear, and she is happy to say it if she thinks it would fix whatever issue they may be having, no matter how true it may be. Her constant cheerful, bubbly voice allows her lies to have an air of sweet genuineness that makes them all the more appealing. She is not past manipulating the people around her if she thinks that, in the end, her manipulation will leave them happier than before. She has been trying to manipulate herself into thinking everything is okay for years, so she's definitely got some experience under her belt. However, she does not really use this to do things that would benefit only herself or to get her way; she genuinely believes her lies are another way of helping the people around her.
3:26 AM
STREETS: Roma has always been very in tune with other people's emotions, and that has only become more true since she stopped paying as much to her own. However, Roma always gives people the benefit of the doubt and always believes in peoples' ability to improve, even when it isn't particularly earned. She likes to believe in the good of the people around her, even if it makes her naive. SMARTS: She is incredibly curious about the world around her! She does legitimately enjoy learning about the many jobs she picks up, and learning about as many things as she can is able to serve as another distraction to not think about The Situation. She is definitely not the brightest though; having lived on the Tracks her whole life, she never had much of a formal education. She lacks a lot of knowledge that would be common sense to most other people, especially Mainlanders. Learning and remembering things during work is a conscious effort and does not come naturally to her. TOUGH: She has struggled with chronic dizziness since she was young, but it has become a lot less severe as she has gotten older and learned how to better manage it. However, it does cause her to have to sometimes step away from her work and, on rare occasions, it can be so intense that she is unable to get up and walk for an extended period of time. This chronic illness is one reason why Emerson was so doting towards her and why he had no real inhibitions about scavenging; it meant he could better take care of the both of them. Her immune system is generally in order, however it has definitely weakened in the last few years due to intense internalized stress. She is not particularly accident prone.
3:26 AM
WEIRD: Roma likes to believe that maybe someone out there is looking out for her and could maybe one day bring Emerson home. However, this belief has very much dulled as more and more time has passed. She is not particularly spiritual or knowledgeable about anything supernatural, and her knowledge of the world really only extends to wherever the Tracks community reaches.
