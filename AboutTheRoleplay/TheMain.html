THE TEMPLATE NAME: AGE: HEIGHT: OCCUPATION: RESIDENCY: REPUTATION: STATS COOL: How calm are you under pressure? Can you command a room with your presence? CHARM: How easy is it for you to manipulate someone? To get your way? STREETS: Are you able to recognize people's intentions? Can you read a bad situation well? SMARTS: Do you have a curiosity that needs to be satiated? Are you good at deducing based on your logic? TOUGH: Does your skin break easily? Are you prone to sickness? WEIRD: How strong is your supernatural sense? Is your existence entwined with the world? These are the questions you should keep in mind when deciding on values for each stat. Before you send in a character, please use your personal channels to discuss with the hosts about your stats. (edited)
